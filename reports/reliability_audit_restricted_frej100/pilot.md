## Reject-margin bounds


F5 passes when `coverage_full >= 90%` (narrow interval) or `unconditional_coverage >= 90%` (widened interval). The widened interval is a valid, conservative 95% confidence interval under the identification assumptions; the narrow interval is not.

The narrow interval is a **partial-identification interval conditional on the P0 estimate**, not a confidence interval. Coverage of the true full-population value is a diagnostic for the narrow interval: a narrow interval on a noisy P0 estimate can miss. The incumbent's narrow interval covers the true full value in n/a of replications. Do not interpret the bounds as 95% coverage.
