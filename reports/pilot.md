## Limitations

- The observation mechanism and review capacity are simulated, not observed.
- The cost matrix is a normalized assumption, not a production estimate.
- The reject margin is bounded, not point-identified.
- BAF is synthetic; no Philippine bank data is used.
- The audit design is a post-hoc verification of approved applications; no random approvals/rejections.
- This is a pilot, not a production system or a causal deployment claim.
